# Instant signup emails

Two transactional emails are created when `/api/instant/brief/` receives a complete, validated brief from a server-confirmed Stripe trial/subscription and receives a durable CRM receipt. Neither opening checkout nor completing checkout without the final brief sends them.

| Message | Recipient | Reply-To | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer confirmation | Step-one signup email | `info@webm8agency.com` | Signup received; website within one business day; delivery by email and WhatsApp; unlimited revisions; actual trial end and billing portal. |
| Internal build brief | `INSTANT_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL`, currently `info@webm8agency.com` | Customer signup email | Full business and website brief, links, public contact choices, separately labelled private delivery contacts, CRM and billing references. |

Both use `WebM8 <hello@webm8agency.com>`, the existing verified sending domain. HTML and plain-text versions are generated by `lib/instantEmail.ts`. User content is HTML-escaped, subjects cannot contain line breaks, and email tracking is not introduced.

## Delivery and recovery

Migration `003_instant_email_outbox.sql` creates the outbox in the dedicated Instant database. Both immutable email payloads are inserted in the same transaction as the CRM receipt, with a unique `(order_id, kind)` constraint. Existing completed signups are not backfilled.

The brief route uses Next.js `after()` to send after returning the success response. A failed email never undoes a successful signup. Each message has independent status and a two-minute lease. A deterministic Resend idempotency key handles lost responses; successful provider IDs are retained.

The `instant-signup-email-retry` Vercel Schedule targets the protected POST `/api/instant/email-retry/` every 15 minutes, with the Hobby plan's 15-minute jitter. It catches failed sends and expired worker leases without requiring the customer to return. `CRON_SECRET` must be a production-only secret of at least 32 characters. The API returns counts only; it never returns recipient data or email contents.

Transient errors back off; permanent provider rejections become `failed`. After twelve attempts, or before the provider's 24-hour idempotency window can expire, uncertain messages become `needs_review` rather than risking duplicate delivery. These states emit an `instant-email: attention required` error. Operators can inspect outbox metadata and the Resend message history before any manual retry; never reset an uncertain old send without checking whether it was accepted.

Required production settings: `INSTANT_EMAILS_ENABLED=1`, `RESEND_API_KEY` (domain-restricted sending access), `INSTANT_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL`, `CRON_SECRET`, and the existing Stripe portal and database configuration. No production sender secret is configured for preview. Disabled email mode is a no-op. The billing prebuild check verifies the queue table and sender/worker configuration.
