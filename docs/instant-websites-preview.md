# Instant Websites: signup prototype

Confirmed product direction: £0 at signup with a 10-day free trial starting at completed checkout, followed by £39/month. Customers can cancel anytime with no cancellation fee; cancelling before the trial ends avoids the first charge. Unlimited revisions until 100% satisfaction are included. These owner-confirmed terms supersede the earlier upfront charge, seven-day refund window and one-revision allowance. The offer remains for one-page local-business sites with enquiries and booking requests; ecommerce/payment systems require a custom quote. No setup fee and one small ongoing content request per month remain in the preview package.

## Preview boundary

With billing disabled (the default and current public deployment), no payment fields, Stripe calls, CRM writes, email sends, remote uploads or live order states are used. The prototype checkout advances only after the proposed terms have been acknowledged; it is explicitly a simulation. Completion states say nothing has been sent. Downloads are marked as prototype briefs. Assets stay in memory and are not included in downloads.

Answers are saved to sessionStorage, under a versioned key, for 24 hours since the last save. They stay in the current browser tab, do not enter analytics or URLs, and can be cleared through an inline confirmation. Draft parsing bounds values, filters unknown options and restores only reachable screens. Storage failure preserves in-memory answers and shows a warning; browser departure warns when selected files or unsaved answers would be lost. Secure email return links are a production task, not implemented here.
