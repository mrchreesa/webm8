Homepage drops

Two directions for the WebM8 homepage, built as working prototypes with the real copy, plans and portfolio. Open each one on a computer and on your phone. Type, drag, click and scroll: the interactions are the point.

Main Street

Drop A

Warm, personal, close to today's brand. The visitor types their business name and watches a website get built for them, live.

Moment
Pick a trade and the preview breaks back to a wireframe, then rebuilds in that trade's colors with the visitor's name in the logo and web address.
Also
Clickable anatomy of a real client site, a drag-to-compare before and after, a review report that ticks itself off, and an octopus that watches your cursor.
Palette
Paper, navy, orange for actions
Type
Archivo Expanded for headlines, Geist for text

After Hours

Drop B, chosen

Dark, cinematic, more premium. The visitor picks their trade and can add their business name. Scrolling then plays one of their customers' journeys, from a local search to the owner's phone lighting up.

Moment
The phone types their trade's search, opens a site with their name on it and fills in the booking or quote form, then spins around in 3D to show the owner's lock screen and the new request. 15 trades, plus "something else". Ads can pre-select one with ?trade=hvac.
Also
A 3D fan of the five live sites, "Eight arms. One team." with the mascot linked to everything included, and a timeline that lights up as you read.
Palette
Night navy, mascot blue, neon for actions
Type
Funnel Display for headlines, Geist for text
