WEBM8 FACEBOOK LAUNCH KIT

1. Upload 01-profile-cream.png as your profile picture.
   1080 × 1080 PNG. The original octopus is centered for a circular crop.
   Optional alternative: 01-profile-navy-alternative.png.

2. Upload 02-cover.png as your cover/background.
   1640 × 924 PNG, approximately 16:9. Keep the image centered in the crop
   preview and check both desktop and phone after saving.
   If your Page presents a shallower banner, try 02-cover-wide-alternative.png
   (1640 × 624). These are alternative exports: upload only one cover.
   Main text sits in the central band to tolerate a shallower phone crop.
   Facebook layouts vary; the included crop preview is illustrative.

3. Open PAGE-COPY.txt for your bio, About description, services, website,
   email, suggested username, action-button destination, captions, alt text,
   and optional Messenger replies.

4. Publish the welcome graphic and matching caption, then pin the post.
   Follow with the services and free-review posts, for example a few days apart.
   All three feed graphics are 1080 × 1080 PNGs.

5. Upload the Story graphic (1080 × 1920) when you want to promote the review.
   Key content leaves space at the top and bottom for Story interface controls.
   Add a real link sticker if that option is available; the image button and
   printed web address are visual elements and are not clickable by themselves.

OPEN-ME.html is a local gallery with image download links.
package-preview.png is a contact sheet, not a graphic to upload.
profile-circle-preview.png and cover-mobile-crop-preview.png are QA previews.

BRANDING
Original mascot: public/mascot.png, used as supplied.
Typeface: Geist, embedded in the editable HTML from the website’s font asset.
Navy #0E2F56 / deep navy #071A33 / warm cream #F8F5F0
Lime badge #D4FF35 / orange accent #F4681F / action orange #C8500F
Logo treatment follows the website’s Web + lime M8 badge.
Messaging and contact information come from lib/site.ts, lib/seo.ts,
the home page, and the About and Contact pages in the supplied website.

EDITABLE FILES
editable-source/ contains self-contained HTML layouts with embedded mascot
and font assets. Each opens offline in a browser at its original canvas size.
build.cjs can regenerate the exports in the website project using Sharp and
Playwright, with PLAYWRIGHT_PATH pointing to an installed Playwright package.
It uses the website’s existing mascot and cached Geist Latin font.
export-checks.json records image sizes, font readiness, and loading checks.

FACEBOOK SIZING REFERENCE
Meta’s reference: https://www.facebook.com/help/125379114252045
The help page required login when checked on 19 September 2026. Current
published guidance describing the 16:9 desktop and 2.4:1 phone presentation:
https://socialmagnum.com/blog/facebook-cover-photo-size
Final crop placement should always be checked in your own Page’s preview.

This package prepares assets and text; it does not publish or change your Page.
